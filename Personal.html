In my personal photography I work to bring the experience I have whilst taking photographs into the final image; for me this is usually a time of reflection.
Working mostly in long term photobook projects, I use photography to explore the narrative found in sets of images through pairings and sequences.


I avoid seeking social or political agendas and focus on the feeling of images first and foremost.
